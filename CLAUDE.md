# Abernethy Century Campaign — Voice & Messaging

## Core message: from the community, for the community

This is **not** a "school asks parents for money for kids" fundraiser. It's a neighborhood reinvesting in a shared institution. The community should see the school as **theirs** — not just the families with kids enrolled.

**Don't** frame the campaign as parents/donors → kids. Avoid copy like "Built by us, for our kids" or anything that implies the beneficiaries are only children. It excludes alumni, neighbors, retirees, future families, and anyone in the area who values having a strong elementary school next door.

**Do** frame it as community → community. The school belongs to the whole neighborhood. Everyone benefits — kids get a great place to learn, the neighborhood gets a vibrant cornerstone, the city gets a 100-year institution to keep going.

Examples that work: "Built together. Shared by all." / "By the neighborhood, for the neighborhood." / "This school belongs to all of us."
